Live weather for every city on Earth

WeatherPulse brings you the weather today — live, as it happens — for any city in the world. Search a city and get the current temperature, what it actually feels like, an hourly temperature graph for the next 24 hours, and a complete 7-day forecast with daily highs, lows and rain chances.

Every detail that decides your day is here in one place: rain probability, wind speed and direction, humidity, UV index, air pressure, visibility, cloud cover and exact sunrise and sunset times. Data is refreshed live from the Open-Meteo weather service every time you open WeatherPulse or search a new city — no account, no sign-up, completely free.
